About Gide

Gide is a growing family of apps, books, and guides created to help people make better decisions, stay safer, build better habits, and use AI with more confidence.

What Gide Is

One brand built around guidance, support, and protection.

Gide was created by Gide to provide practical tools for real life. The goal is simple: help people live wiser, work smarter, stay safer, and move forward with confidence.

The Gide family includes mobile apps for safety, calm, faith, and progress. It also includes plain-English AI books and resources for people who want to understand and use AI without feeling overwhelmed.

Gide is not about hype. It is about practical help, clear guidance, and useful tools people can use right away.

The Gide App Family

Support for different parts of life.

Each Gide app has a clear purpose. Together, they create a simple support system for everyday needs.

Gide Guard

Helps users think through safety concerns, recognize suspicious situations, and stay more aware of scams, fraud, and risk.

Gide Calm

Provides simple calming tools, reflections, and support for stressful moments when users need to pause and reset.

Gide Faith

Offers scripture, prayer, reflection, and faith-based encouragement for daily spiritual growth.

Gide Stride

Encourages movement, progress, and personal growth by helping users turn steps and goals into visible progress.

Gide Legacy

Preserves a family's voices, photos, stories, and relationships in a private archive the family controls.

Gide Roots

Builds a private family tree that organizes the people, relationships, and generations behind those stories.

The AI Book Series

Plain-English guides for real life and work.

Gide also includes practical AI books and downloadable resources for people who want to use AI wisely, safely, and productively.

AI at Work

A practical guide to using AI to save time, solve problems, boost productivity, and grow your career.

AI for Everyday Life

Simple ways to use AI at home, at work, and in daily decision-making.

The AI Gold Rush

A clear look at the hype, money, and opportunity behind today’s AI boom.

AI Wayfinder

A short guide designed to help readers find clarity in the age of AI.

Our Approach

Clear, practical, and built for real people.

Gide is built around privacy, clarity, usefulness, and trust. The goal is to create tools that are easy to use and easy to understand.

Many Gide tools are designed to work without requiring users to give up more information than necessary. The focus is on practical support, not collecting personal data.

Whether someone is reading a guide, opening an app, or looking for a quick answer, Gide aims to provide help that feels clear, calm, and useful.

Looking Ahead

Gide is still growing.

New apps, books, tools, and resources may be added over time as the Gide family expands.

The long-term goal is to build a trusted collection of simple digital tools that help people live wiser, work smarter, stay safer, and move forward with confidence.
