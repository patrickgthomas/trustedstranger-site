Classic Sudoku, made comfortable

Think it through. Enjoy the moment.

Hints that teach

Read the next logical step in plain English. Tap again to fill it in, or work it out yourself.

Five ways to challenge yourself

Choose Gentle, Easy, Steady, Sharp, or Expert. Every puzzle is checked for one unique solution.

A little jewel magic

Swap the numbers for nine colorful jewels. Keep small numbers visible if you want a familiar guide.

Your daily puzzle

Try the same daily puzzle as other players. The week starts gently and grows more challenging.

Room to change your mind

Use notes, erase a number, or undo a move. Optional mistake marking helps you find your way.

Quiet by choice

Turn sounds and animations off whenever you prefer. Your progress and settings stay on your device.

Simple rules. Plenty to discover.

Fill every row, column, and 3×3 box with the numbers 1–9, using each number once. In Jewel mode, the same rules apply to the nine jewels.

When can I download it?

Gide Sudoku is being prepared for its first App Store release. A download link will be added when it is available.

Do I need an account?

No. The game works offline and saves progress locally on your device.

Where do I get help?
