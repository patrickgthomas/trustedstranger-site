Introduction

Gide is a family of applications, websites, books, guides, and resources designed to provide guidance, support, education, encouragement, safety awareness, and personal well-being tools.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Gide websites, applications, downloads, and related services operated by Gide.

Information We Collect

We strive to collect as little personal information as possible. Many Gide applications are designed to function without requiring users to create an account.

Depending on the application, website, or feature used, information may include:

  • App preferences and settings
  • Saved favorites, bookmarks, or progress
  • Notification preferences
  • Theme, accessibility, and display preferences
  • Basic diagnostic, crash, or performance information
  • Information you choose to submit through a contact form
  • Email address, if you choose to contact us or sign up for updates

Much of this information may be stored locally on your device and used only to provide app functionality.

Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Unless specifically required by a feature you choose to use, we do not intentionally collect:

  • Your phone number
  • Your precise location
  • Your contacts
  • Financial account information
  • Government identification numbers
  • Health records or medical files
  • Passwords or login credentials for other services

Contact Forms and Email

If you submit a contact form or send us an email, we may receive your name, email address, message, and any other information you choose to include.

We use this information to respond to your message, provide support, answer questions, and improve Gide resources and services.

Gide — Legacy: Additional Privacy Details

Where your memories are stored. Your memories — stories, photos, video, audio, and documents — are stored on your device and, if you choose to connect it, in your own Google Drive account. Gide does not host, store, or have access to the contents of your memories on our servers. The local vault is stored inside the application’s private storage and protected by your phone’s operating-system security. Gide does not use a separate encryption key, passphrase, or recovery phrase.

Device permissions. With your permission, Gide — Legacy uses your camera, microphone, and photo/file library solely to let you capture and add memories. This content is saved only to your vault; it is not sent to us.

Google Sign-In. If you use family sharing, you sign in with Google via Firebase Authentication. We receive a Google-issued account identifier to establish your identity across your devices. We never receive your Google password.

Google Drive. If you connect Google Drive, Gide — Legacy accesses only the files it creates using the drive.file scope to store and share your memories. It cannot see the rest of your Drive. Sync also stores one complete private vault backup in a Gide backup folder owned by your Google account. That backup is not shared with family members. Connecting the same account after reinstalling lets Gide offer to restore the backup.

Family sharing. When you create or join a Shared Family Vault, we store membership information only — your chosen display name, your role, a link (URL) to the memories you publish, seat/membership counts, and invitation codes. We do not store your actual memories, photos, audio, or documents. Memories you mark as “shared” are published to your own Google Drive. Gide removes anonymous link access and grants access only to the verified Google accounts on the approved family roster. A removed family member may retain a copy they previously chose to save to their own vault.

Purchases. The one-time Family License and optional membership packs are processed by Google Play and managed through RevenueCat. We receive confirmation of your purchase status; we never receive or store your payment card details.

On-device processing. Text recognition from photos and speech-to-text run entirely on your device. This content is not sent to us.

Deleting your data. In Gide — Legacy, open Settings, then choose Delete my account and local data. This deletes your Firebase Authentication identity, Gide membership and coordination records, and the local vault on that phone. If you are the family organizer, deleting the account also closes that family’s Gide service. Other members’ local and Google Drive files are not erased. You may also submit a deletion request through our account deletion page.

User-owned and retained records. Files in your own Google Drive remain under your control and can be deleted from Google Drive. Account deletion removes Gide's RevenueCat customer record. Google Play may retain transaction records required for purchases, refunds, accounting, fraud prevention, and legal compliance. Uninstalling the app removes its local vault unless you have a Google Drive or exported ZIP backup.

Third-party services. Family features rely on Google Sign-In, Google Drive, Google Play billing, Firebase, and RevenueCat. Each has its own privacy policy.
