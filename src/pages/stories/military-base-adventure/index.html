Meet Ethan and his moving crew.

Seven-year-old Ethan Ellis is moving to a new military base with his mom, Staff Sergeant Sarah Ellis, his golden retriever Scout, and his well-loved teddy bear, Sergeant Teddy.

Saying goodbye is hard. With new places to explore and new friends to meet, Ethan begins to discover that home is also the love and memories he carries with him.

This picture book is being developed for military children and their families. Publication details will be shared here when they are ready.
